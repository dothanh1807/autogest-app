Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app AutoGest handles your information.

The short version
  • The app works fully offline. It does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
  • We have no servers, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics SDKs.
  • The Accessibility service only reads the screen and acts while a script you started is running. Screen content is processed on your device and is not stored or shared.
  • Everything you create (scripts, images, schedules, run history) is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

1. Accessibility service (AccessibilityService API)

AutoGest lets you build or record automation scripts: sequences of taps, swipes, text input and other actions that the app replays for you. To do this it uses Android’s Accessibility service. You must turn the service on yourself in the system Settings, after reading an in-app disclosure that explains what it does. When it is on, the app:

  • Reads the content shown on screen — text, content descriptions and positions of elements in the app in front of you — to find where a step should tap, or to check a “text” condition in your script.
  • Takes screenshots (Android 11 and newer) only when your script contains an image tap, image wait or color check step, to compare the screen with the image or color you chose. Screenshots are held in memory only for the comparison and discarded immediately; they are never saved to storage.
  • Performs actions on your behalf — tap, long press, swipe, scroll, type text, press Back, Home, Recent apps and open the apps you picked — exactly as your script’s steps say.
  • Records your gestures when you use the recorder, so they can become script steps. Nothing is added to a script until you review and accept it.

The service does not read the screen, take screenshots or act when no script is running and you are not recording. We do not use the Accessibility service to collect personal or sensitive data, to read passwords, to change your settings behind your back, or for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We never receive a copy.

DataWhat it containsWhy
Scripts Script names, steps, screen coordinates, text you want typed, and — when you record or enable “prefer element matching” — the text or content description of the element you tapped To replay your automation, and to find the same element again even if the layout moves
Template images A downscaled copy of images you pick with the system photo picker for image steps To find that image on screen
Colors The color of the single pixel you pick for a color check step To wait for that color
Run history The last 200 runs: script name, start and end time, how the run ended So you can see what ran and whether it succeeded
Schedules Script, time of day and days of the week you chose To start scripts at the time you set
Settings and consent Your preferences, and the version and time you agreed to the Accessibility disclosure To remember your choices and show the disclosure again if it changes

3. Permissions and how they are used

Accessibility service Described in section 1.
Display over other apps Shows the floating control bubble, the run progress banner and the recorder / coordinate picker overlays on top of other apps.
Notifications Shows a notification with Pause and Stop buttons while a script runs, and a “missed schedule” notice when a scheduled run could not start.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) Starts scheduled scripts on time. Without it the app uses a less precise alarm.
Run at startup Re-arms your schedules after the phone restarts, the app is updated or the time zone changes.
List of launchable apps Shows the apps installed on your phone that have a launcher icon so you can pick one for an “Open app” step or as the recording target. This list is not stored or sent anywhere.
Storage (Android 9 and older only) Saves an exported script file to the Downloads folder when you ask for it. Newer Android versions need no storage permission for this.

For scheduled runs the app may turn the screen on and dismiss a lock screen that has no security (swipe). It never bypasses a PIN, pattern, password or biometric lock; in that case the run is skipped and you get a notification instead.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no Internet permission, no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call log.

5. Sharing a script

A script leaves your phone only when you choose to export or share it. The exported .autoscript file contains the script’s steps and any template images it uses. You pick where it goes (Downloads folder, a messaging app, email…), and that destination’s own privacy policy applies. Importing a file someone sent you only reads that file.

6. Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Depending on your device settings, Google may collect crash and performance reports (Android vitals) and share aggregated statistics with us through the Play Console. If in-app purchases are added in the future, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play Billing; we will never see your payment details. This processing is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete scripts, schedules and history inside the app, clear everything with Settings > Apps > AutoGest > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. The app has no network access, so it cannot leak your data over the Internet.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will change with each update. If the Accessibility service starts reading new kinds of data, the app will show the disclosure again and ask for your consent before continuing.
